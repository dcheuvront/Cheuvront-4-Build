What are Warframes?

Warframes are powerful suits used by the Tenno in the world of Warframe. Each Warframe has its own unique appearance, abilities, and strengths that influence how it performs during missions. Roles describe the different ways a Warframe can contribute, such as dealing damage, supporting teammates, controlling enemies, using stealth, or surviving difficult situations. These roles matter because they give players different ways to approach missions, work together as a team, and choose a playstyle that fits their preferences. Some Warframes can even perform multiple roles, making them versatile in different situations.

Roles of Warframes

  • DPS

    Deals high damage to enemies.

  • Support

    Assists teammates with healing, buffs, and other supportive abilities.

  • Control

    Manipulates enemies with crowd control abilities, such as stunning, freezing, or slowing them.

  • Stealth

    Excels at remaining hidden and avoiding detection by enemies.

  • Survival

    Excels at staying alive in difficult situations, often through self-healing or damage mitigation.
